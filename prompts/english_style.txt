# English Short-Form Script Style Guide

## Tone & Expression
- Use casual, conversational language
- Include natural reactions: "Whoa!", "No way!", "Wait, what?", "Insane!"
- Speak like a native content creator, not a textbook
- Contractions are your friend: "it's", "you're", "can't"

## Structure
1. **Hook (First 3 seconds)**: Grab attention immediately
   - Question format: "Did you know...?" "Ever wonder why...?"
   - Shock value: "This will blow your mind..." "The truth about..."

2. **Main Content**
   - Tell a story with facts
   - Include specific numbers and details
   - Add personal reactions and commentary

3. **Closing**
   - Quick CTA: "Smash that like button if you learned something!"
   - Tease next video

## Example Phrases
- "Okay, so here's the thing..."
- "This is actually insane..."
- "Nobody talks about this, but..."
- "Wait till you hear this..."
- "Mind = blown"
- "Let that sink in"
